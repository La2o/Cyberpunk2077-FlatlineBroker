# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Fixed: flatlines could not be sold at most shops players found. 1.0 bought them only at a hand-picked list of vendors, half of which turned out to be skill trainers or food stalls, and nothing told players which shops counted. Now every Junk Vendor (the junk-shop icon on the map) buys them.
- Item descriptions now say where to sell: "sell it at any junk shop".

## 1.1.0

- Optional Wannabe Edgerunner integration: with WE installed, each recorded flatline costs extra humanity (1 per grade) on top of WE's own kill cost. Without WE nothing changes.
- Fixed wording: the BD Scroller costs 4 cyberware capacity, not "humanity".

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Zetatech BD Scroller frontal-cortex cyberware, stocked by 16 ripperdocs.
- Kill recording: Street Hit, Ghost, Snuff Reel, Corpo Exit and Psycho Takedown flatlines in three grades.
- Flatlines sell only to BD dealers, and never through sell-all-junk or disassemble-junk.
- NCPD patrol scans and the manifest rule raise heat while you carry flatlines.
